A line diff is not enough.

PatchLab records behavior, scope, permissions, capabilities, and integrity. The same standard applies to human and AI-assisted changes.

Reproduce first

Require the defect to fail on the base revision and pass on the candidate revision.

Review new powers

Detect permission, network, dependency, secret, and workflow changes.

Carry the evidence

Export JSON, Markdown, SARIF, and a deterministic archive with SHA-256.

Trust the base policy

A candidate patch cannot silently weaken the rules used to judge itself.

Protect the output

Bounded archives, safe paths, atomic writes, and strict verification reduce evidence tampering.

Keep people in control

PatchLab can block or request review. It does not merge or approve code.
