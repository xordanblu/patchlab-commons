# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a technical community where people can contribute without harassment or intimidation.

Expected behavior includes:

- direct and respectful technical feedback;
- clear separation between a person and the quality of a change;
- honest disclosure of limitations and conflicts;
- protection of private security information;
- patience with new contributors and language differences.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, or targeted insults;
- sexualized attention;
- publication of private information;
- deliberate disruption;
- fabricated evidence, metrics, identities, or security claims;
- pressure to merge a change without review.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers. Maintainers can remove content, close contributions, restrict participation, or ban an account. Decisions should be proportional and documented privately.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, events, and official communication channels.
